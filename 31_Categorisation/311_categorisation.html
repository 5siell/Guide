For every template I will be having the notation:

  • territories x armies per initial territory

  • neutrals/neutrals in distribution

  • wastelands/wasteland size

  • base income

For example, for Strat MME the notation is 3x4, neutrals: 2/4, wastelands: 7/10, base: +5.

As of April of 2024, the MTL consists of 51 Templates. For the sake of linguistic cohesion one must categorise these by finding common features that make 2 or more templates play out in a similar way. Bare in mind that some templates might belong in 2 categories, like Strategic Commanders being WR and a commander template. In such occasions I have weighted arbitrarily the one trait of the template is is most defining. Thus, the outline is (Table 1):

  • 19 Vanilla SR templates

  • 9 WR templates

  • 23 Case-specific templates
