\section{Categories} \label{Categories}

For every template I will be having the notation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item territories x armies per initial territory
    \item neutrals/neutrals in distribution
    \item wastelands/wasteland size
    \item base income
\end{itemize}

For example, for Strat MME the notation is 3x4, neutrals: 2/4, wastelands: 7/10, base: +5.

As of April of 2024, the MTL consists of 51 Templates. For the sake of linguistic cohesion one must categorise these by finding common features that make 2 or more templates play out in a similar way. Bare in mind that some templates might belong in 2 categories, like Strategic Commanders being WR and a commander template. In such occasions I have weighted arbitrarily the one trait of the template is is most defining. Thus, the outline is (Table \ref{templates}):

\begin{itemize}
    \item 19 Vanilla SR templates
    \item 9 WR templates 
    \item 23 Case-specific templates
\end{itemize}
