Relative to the section on Ratios and Kill Rates (Table [table:killrate_variants] and following bullet-points at [Reverse_Kill_Rates]), 0.75/0.50 KR transform the game into a fast pace, attacking based game. The attacker’s advantage is so big, you’d rather defend through out-delaying and attacking back. Chokepoints don’t hold and safe income is only safe if it’s remote; the game is about initiatives -> fast Income (FTBs - max Income T2/T3) and hitting first.

At 75% KR for the attacker, every four armies you add the fractional part is repeated, as seen in Table 1. Residue 0/ Multiples of 4 are perfectly efficient (Just like 5 attacks in 60/70 KR). Order of efficiency is 2>1>0>3, with efficiency equalizing at roughly 14-15 attackers.

Defender Kill-Rates at 50% are very simple.

  • Even number of defenders -> exact 0.5×m0.5\times m Kills.

  • Odd number of defenders -> Always above 0.5×m0.5\times m - more efficient. (e.g. 3->2 (0.667). But you are still often better served attacking your own territories to defend. (5 defenders from 1 army stand guard and 4 deployments kill 3 attackers. If you out-delay and attack your defending territory with 2x 2 army attacks - the 4 deployments - you kill 4 attackers.)
