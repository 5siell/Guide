\subsubsection{Fast Earth}

\paragraph{Outline}

Same with Timid Lands. No +1 RFs and different map where safe income matters more often.

\paragraph{Picking, Gameplay and Different Strategies}

This template is identical to Timid Lands with the exception of the +1 RF cards. As such, the couple of things are to be noted:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The gameplay ideas and principles that hold true for Timid Lands also apply here. (check that section).
    \item Some of the combos that are applicable in Timid Lands are an army sort here. (For example in Timid Lands you can get a +4 with 2 picks by end of Turn 2 even if it has a wasteland, with a tap from the RF card. In Fast Earth you cannot).
    \item The Modified Medium Earth (in comparison to Timid Lands):
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item is bigger in territory size 129 vs 119 territories,
        \item has more bonuses in 27vs24,
        \item is compartmentalized in continents/regions, including bottlenecks and distances you cannot often cover so as to counter a bonus. 
    \end{enumerate}
    Therefore, safe income and positional advantage is slightly more valuable in Fast Earth.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Picking:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item If you see a +4 FTB idk probably cover it cause overextending for it isn't worth it. \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=41785096}{But I found this game so..}
    \item +3 FTB -> Pick or cover. Ignore only if you have a plan to counter it's expansion or out-expand on T3/T4.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Gameplay} 

The above Table (\ref{fast_earth}) doesn't include all progressions, but some ideas. In MME the bonuses are well separated into big regions and continents, so you really should evaluate safe income above fast but volatile income. Bonuses in Asia like CR/WR/Caucasus are very high risk high reward and even if you get an advantage in income such as \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=36940240}{here}, you need pitch-perfect predictions to survive.
\\
\\
Fast Earth's gameplay is similar to Timid Lands in the way that if you are ahead, you play to convert with first moves and beneficial attacking rates, whereas if you are on behind you need tricks to come back. To get ahead you need an army advantage, which is gained mainly from mind-games and manipulating the move order. Take for example \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=37803798}{this game}, where on Turn 4 I use OP to secure first order so that I can out-delay in Indonesia through eliminating the leftovers in Greenland. Then on Turn 6 Crazy does the same using OP so that he can out-delay in Indonesia and recapture back. I went in just 1 turn from a winning position to a losing position from a ridiculous swing. 
\\
\\
Some bonuses like Middle East and North Africa, that were inefficient in Strat MME, are as efficient as every other bonus here, so beware of that. North Africa is an excellent positional pick (double border vs EAf, Mid East, safe vs SA and Eu). Respectively, Middle East as the 2nd or 3rd bonus on T2/T3 is awesome.
