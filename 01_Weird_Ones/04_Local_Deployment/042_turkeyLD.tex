My first tip for Turkey LD is to take proper care of your picks.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Don't gamble everything on a 1st pick. Accommodate for losing first pick either through 2/3, 6th pick, or the fact you will have first order on Turn 1. For reference, \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=40836522}{here} the center is clustered and you cannot pick elsewhere. Avoid the brawl -> loss. Our 2/3 in case of mirroring 1/2/3 can just take the red +2 FTB, as losing 1st pick means first move T1. 
    \item Similarly \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=41524702}{here}, 6 in a neighboring adjacent territory allows me a transfer in with priority T1.
\end{itemize}

The second thing and equally as important is to play the best moves in your position. When behind/equal, you need a good trade, a good exchange, a positional maneuver or you need. When ahead look to convert the game into a win. 

\begin{itemize}
    \item Transfer-only is incredible for making sure a move goes through only in case your opponent hasn't attacked.
    \item Attack-only is great for setting up eliminations or moving armies over the board conditionally. Ir the attack goes through, you attack, if you already captured, then the attack doesn't go through and you keep your armies in a more important territory.
    \item Don't waste armies on taps that aren't guaranteed 1:1 trade. Well unless you are losing and desperate.
    \item Play for kill-rates. Optimize every move within 1-2 or more turns in advance. 
\end{itemize}
