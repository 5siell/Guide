\subsection{Local Deployment}

Local Deployment (commonly named as LD) is a setting where Income is tagged to the Bonus that generated it, as in +2 from "Thrace" can only be deployed in Thrace and it's territories (Base Income and Card Deployments are still Global).
\\
\\
LD drastically rewires gameplay and the core strategy loop in a lot of ways (compared to vanilla SR 0\% luck).
\begin{itemize}
    \item OD/OP are even stronger. OD decides the outcome between mirror stacks. OP snipes smaller stacks to get better kill-rates.
    \item Clusters of Bonuses are stronger than isolated and remote/safe bonuses. The remote bonus cannot supply with armies the front fast and loses. Airlifts somehow balance this out.
    \item Gameplay is all about the map dynamics. If you have a 10vs10 income equal armies position where you bring armies to the front faster than the opponent does, you win. From that perspective and if you look at LD as a puzzle, a forced win involves picking in the biggest and most rich income regions and out-expanding your opponent.
    \item Precisely because you send armies to a front from 3,4,10 territories behind the fog, the games drag out as players don't realize they are dead lost and play on for a dozen turns clueless that the game is over.
    \item LD is very punishing. Micromanaging matters a lot as you can get small advantages and build from there. In contrast, LD is also very noob-friendly as it has less degrees of freedom on picks and fairly straight forward gameplay. It is weird in that sense where the curve of excellence isn't as steep as Strat MME, but micro-managing your army gains matters a lot (look up Transfer-only, Attack-only, \% moves and kill-rates).
\end{itemize}
