"Weird Settings"

Extra armies every 2 territories

Succession Wars features extra armies in 5 territories but is listed in the LD templates. Nonetheless the ideas here apply there.

The setting "extra armies every 2 territories" takes place in French Brawl and Yorkshire Brawl (FB & YB), two fully predictable, information and efficiency based templates. The predictable aspect originates from Light Fog (LF), a vital part in the template that gives you information of the Income/Armies situation.

Consider a game like this. Xeno’s moves Turn 5 are only possible because he has to keep up with the +3 threat. Without LF, gameplay changes drastically to something closer to Yin’angzhou.

The efficiency aspect comes from the army & territory settings. In the game you get Income two ways:

  1. Through Bonuses (n-2, bigger bonuses way more efficient but at the same time slower).

  2. Through Territories, Table 1.
