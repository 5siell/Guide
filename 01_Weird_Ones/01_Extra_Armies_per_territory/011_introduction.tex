\section{"Weird Settings"}
\subsection{Extra armies every 2 territories}

\textit{Succession Wars features extra armies in 5 territories but is listed in the LD templates. Nonetheless the ideas here apply there}.
\\
\\
The setting "extra armies every 2 territories" takes place in French Brawl and Yorkshire Brawl (FB \& YB), two fully predictable, information and efficiency based templates. The predictable aspect originates from Light Fog (LF), a vital part in the template that gives you information of the Income/Armies situation.
\\
\\
Consider a game like \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=32826121}{this}. Xeno's moves Turn 5 are only possible because he has to keep up with the +3 threat. Without LF, gameplay changes drastically to something closer to Yin'angzhou.
\\
\\
The efficiency aspect comes from the army \& territory settings. In the game you get Income two ways:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Through Bonuses (n-2, bigger bonuses way more efficient but at the same time slower).
    \item Through Territories, Table \ref{income_fb_yb}.
\end{enumerate}
