Ranking, Elo & Veto Adjustment

r0.35

In regards with Figure [profile_rankings], upon opening your any profile in the MTL you should be able to spot certain numbers/parameters that influence your final ranking, including:

  • Current Rating (CR)

  • Elo Rating (ER)

  • Veto Adjustment (VA)

  • Activity Bonus (AB)

Needless to say, Current Rank is derived when listing all players based on their Current Rating from top to bottom, Best rank is self-explanatory and Best Rating is the highest Current Rating you’ve had till today. The Ladder Rankings follow CR, which is the rounded integer of the following equation: CR=ER+AB+VACR = ER + AB + VA Consequently, the final Elo, or so called Current Ranking, abbreviated as CR or simply Elo later on, consists of 3 parameters.

Elo Rating - ER

The MTL uses a veto-adjusted version of the MWElo Rating System.

Imagine 2 players A and B, with ER equal to r(A) and r(B) respectively before their game. In order to calculate their ER after the match, denoted as r’(A) and r’(B), the algorithm:

  1. Transformation of r to R {R(A)=10(r(a)/400)R(B)=10(r(b)/400)\begin{cases} R(A) = 10^{(r(a)/400)} \\ R(B) = 10^{(r(b)/400)} \end{cases}

  2. Expected Result - E {E(A)=R(A)/(R(A)+R(B))E(B)=R(B)/(R(A)+R(B))\begin{cases} E(A) = R(A)/(R(A)+R(B)) \\ E(B) = R(B)/(R(A)+R(B)) \end{cases}

  3. Final Score - S (Binary) {S(A)={1,ifAwins0,ifAlosesS(B)={0,ifAwins1,ifAloses\begin{cases} S(A) = \begin{cases} 1, \text{if} \; A \; \text{wins} \\ 0, \text{if} \; A \; \text{loses} \end{cases} \\ S(B) = \begin{cases} 0, \text{if} \; A \; \text{wins} \\ 1, \text{if} \; A \; \text{loses} \end{cases} \end{cases}

  4. New Elo - ER {r′(A)=r(A)+K×(S(A)−E(A))r′(B)=r(B)+K×(S(B)−E(B))\begin{cases} r'(A) = r(A) + K \times (S(A)-E(A)) \\ r'(B) = r(B) + K \times (S(B)-E(B)) \\ \end{cases}

Given as an example, for 2 players A and B with ER r(A)=1639r(A) = 1639 and r(B)=1635r(B) = 1635 where player A won (S(A)=1S(A)=1 and S(B)=0S(B) = 0:

  1. Transformation of r to R {R(A)=10(1639/400)=12516.99R(B)=10(1635/400)=12232.07\begin{cases} R(A) = 10^{(1639/400)} = 12516.99 \\ R(B) = 10^{(1635/400)} = 12232.07 \end{cases}

  2. Expected Result - E {E(A)=12516.99)/(12516.99+12232.07)=0.5057562E(B)=12232.07/(12516.99+12232.07)=0.4942438\begin{cases} E(A) = 12516.99)/(12516.99+12232.07) = 0.5057562 \\ E(B) = 12232.07/(12516.99+12232.07) = 0.4942438 \end{cases}

  3. Final Score - S (Binary) {S(A)=1S(B)=0\begin{cases} S(A) = 1 \\ S(B) = 0 \end{cases}

  4. New Elo - ER {r′(A)=1639+32×(1−0.5057562)=1654.816r′(B)=1635+32×(0−0.4942438)=1619.184\begin{cases} r'(A) = 1639 + 32 \times (1-0.5057562) = 1654.816 \\ r'(B) = 1635 + 32 \times (0-0.4942438) = 1619.184 \\ \end{cases}
