In regards with Figure \ref{profile_rankings}, upon opening your any profile in the MTL you should be able to spot certain numbers/parameters that influence your final ranking, including:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Current Rating (CR)
    \item Elo Rating (ER)
    \item Veto Adjustment (VA)
    \item Activity Bonus (AB)
\end{itemize}

Needless to say, Current Rank is derived when listing all players based on their Current Rating from top to bottom, Best rank is self-explanatory and Best Rating is the highest Current Rating you've had till today. The Ladder Rankings follow CR, which is the rounded integer of the following equation:
$$
CR = ER + AB + VA
$$
Consequently, the final Elo, or so called Current Ranking, abbreviated as CR or simply Elo later on, consists of 3 parameters.

\subsubsection{Elo Rating - ER}

The MTL uses a veto-adjusted version of the \href{https://www.warzone.com/Forum/216823-multiday-ladder?Offset=628}{MWElo Rating System}.
\\
\\
Imagine 2 players A and B, with ER equal to r(A) and r(B) respectively before their game. In order to calculate their ER after the match, denoted as r'(A) and r'(B), the algorithm:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Transformation of r to R} $$ \begin{cases}
        R(A) = 10^{(r(a)/400)} \\ 
        R(B) = 10^{(r(b)/400)}
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{Expected Result - E} $$
    \begin{cases}
        E(A) = R(A)/(R(A)+R(B)) \\
        E(B) = R(B)/(R(A)+R(B))
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{Final Score - S (Binary)} $$ 
    \begin{cases}
        S(A) = \begin{cases}
            1, \text{if} \; A \; \text{wins} \\
            0, \text{if} \; A \; \text{loses}
        \end{cases} \\
        S(B) = \begin{cases}
            0, \text{if} \; A \; \text{wins} \\
            1, \text{if} \; A \; \text{loses}
        \end{cases}
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{New Elo - ER} $$
    \begin{cases}
        r'(A) = r(A) + K \times (S(A)-E(A)) \\
        r'(B) = r(B) + K \times (S(B)-E(B)) \\
    \end{cases} $$
\end{enumerate}

Given as an example, for 2 players A and B with ER $r(A) = 1639$ and $r(B) = 1635$ where player A won ($S(A)=1$ and $S(B) = 0$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Transformation of r to R} $$ \begin{cases}
        R(A) = 10^{(1639/400)} = 12516.99 \\ 
        R(B) = 10^{(1635/400)} = 12232.07
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{Expected Result - E} $$
    \begin{cases}
        E(A) = 12516.99)/(12516.99+12232.07) = 0.5057562 \\
        E(B) = 12232.07/(12516.99+12232.07) = 0.4942438
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{Final Score - S (Binary)} $$ 
    \begin{cases}
        S(A) = 1 \\
        S(B) = 0
    \end{cases} $$
    \item \textbf{New Elo - ER} $$
    \begin{cases}
        r'(A) = 1639 + 32 \times (1-0.5057562) = 1654.816 \\
        r'(B) = 1635 + 32 \times (0-0.4942438) = 1619.184 \\
    \end{cases} $$
\end{enumerate}
