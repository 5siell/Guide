\subsection{Reconnaissance}

The recon card appears in 4 templates and is used to get information of your opponent's movements behind the fog and/or spawns. In BIV and GME you cannot use the recon card on Turn 1, but later on, to track your opponent's movements. In Slow Burn you can use it on Turn 1 which effectively means you can pick in a way of planning where you'll recon for information. Lastly, in Macodonia no split it's mostly useful for checking your opponents army stacks.
