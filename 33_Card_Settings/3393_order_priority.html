Order Priority

Order Priority guarantees for you first move in a specific turn. It is generally an incredibly strong card, that only gets stronger at reverse kill-rates.

You can find a lot of scenarios that justify it’s usage which really narrow down to 3 ideas

  • Dodging an opponent’s stack to survive somewhere Turn 12 by Beep. Kind of a desperation move and sometimes high risk but useful in most vanilla and WR templates.

  • Sniping an opponent’s stack of armies before they move / eliminating them. Turn 8. Straight forward idea.

  • Getting Better kill-rates Turn 7. The idea of using order priority to get a beneficial trade is mostly applicable to timid lands and fast earth. However, it can occassionally be helpful in every template

  • Controlling move order. An idea behind this is explained also in Frog’s tips. A niche use of this comes with using the card, and attacking the opponent on your 2nd and 3rd attack, so they go in the same cycle. Another idea with this comes at using the order priority card to move last. For example in this Fast Earth game on Turn 4, I use the card to make sure that a) my first move snipes his armies in Greenland and b) my 2nd move will come after his capture in Indonesia.
