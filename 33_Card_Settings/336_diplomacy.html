Diplomacy

While this does exist in some very niche non-MTL templates, it finds its place only in Slow Burn. It is an incredibly strong card, as it gives you 1 turn of tempo to do whatever you want. Usually you’ll see players, just as illustrated in the link above, to play the card before blockading a choke-point. Other times you can simply use it while ahead in income but behind in armies, as in this game. If you do manage to get the perfect counter as in here, the game is usually won/lost on the spot. Similarly to Ebloc, the existence of this card dictates how you pick and how you use your recon card in Slow Burn.
