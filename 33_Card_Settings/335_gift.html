Gift

On the surface, the gift card has been designed for collaboration in a team-games, however it has a few interesting uses in 1v1s. Namely in:

  • INSS

  • Multi-attack

  • Landria

  • Georgia Army Cap

Starting from the bottom, in Georgia AC you are given 1 Gift card (no pieces per turn) that can be used to gift inactive armies for a 1-turn swing to your opponent. For example in this tour finals game, on turns 6 & 7 both players gift a territory to each other. Similarly in this game gifting the your opponent was about to attack gives a +2 adv.

In Landria, you start with 2 cards and get a new one every 8 turns. Artistically, the card fits the concept of trying to avoid owning adjacent territories in bonuses you don’t own, as in the example game above, both players use the gift card to get short income boosts for one turn.

It’s most overpowerful ability comes in multi attack games and the ability to stop chain-attacks. On this, in Bork as seen on turn 3, you effectively stop the opponent’s stack from interacting, which in combination with army cap is deadly. It finds a niche place in MA BIV on top, in games like where on turn 6 the gift + blockade combo gives you an easy solution to denying a bonus. Besides that, you can use the gift card when defending to in very innovative ways like turn 3, turn 3 and turn 3/4.

Lastly, in INSS templates an interesting case study arises where you can gift a negative territory to an opponent for a short income boost, as discussed in [gift_inss_predictions].
