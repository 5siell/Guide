\subsection{Diplomacy}

While this does exist in some very niche non-MTL templates, it finds its place only in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=40253047}{Slow Burn}. It is an incredibly strong card, as it gives you 1 turn of tempo to do whatever you want. Usually you'll see players, just as illustrated in the link above, to play the card before blockading a choke-point. Other times you can simply use it while ahead in income but behind in armies, as in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=38423302}{this game}. If you do manage to get the perfect counter as in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=41343288}{here}, the game is usually won/lost on the spot. Similarly to Ebloc, the existence of this card dictates how you pick and how you use your recon card in Slow Burn.
