Emergency Blockade

Previously called abandon card, it is one of the most controversial parts of the game. The reason is simply, you press a magic button and your opponent runs his stack into a wall.

There have been a lot of attempts to balance the card, notably it is used in Gotham City although it doesn’t affect too much gameplay. It allows you to be more versatile with picks and cover a combo with a single territory -> press Ebloc, and prevent your opponent from getting the bonus ever.

It’s most accepted implementation comes at Fogless Fighting, a non-MTL but popular template. Balancing it comes with a) both players having an abundance of them and b) Ebloc being at 200% - a relatively low multiplier in an already low income WR template (3 income, n-2 bonuses). The template revolves around safe income and Eblocing the path your opponent has to take to find you, while flanking his.

It finds it’s place in the MTL in Tarabonia’s Choice. The issue comes with being extremely hard to play around it’s existence, where as you can see in the game above, the game is over on Turn 2 just like that. Generally speaking this card is so strong that it’s presence in a template makes the template’s gameplay revolve around Ebloc and income transition/FTBs/counters etc. And you can do tricks like this on Turn 6.
