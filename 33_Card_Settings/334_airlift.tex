\subsection{Airlift}

There are no MTL templates featuring the Airlift Card. Overall in the strategic scene it is considered extremely powerful given it takes place before all moves. The exact sequence as linked in the Turn Phases Section, is Spy-> RF -> Bomb-> E-bloc -> Airlift -> Gift -> attacks/transfers etc.
\\
\\
Nevertheless, airlift is a key card of some very popular QM templates around local deployment and airlifting armies from clusters of income. In AWP, it is used in two templates, firstly in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer/Tournament?ID=47456}{Sumo} for repositioning armies and secondly in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer/Tournament?ID=60562}{Gotham City}. Sumo as a template has a series of issues besides airlift itself, so I'm setting it aside. In Gotham City airlift superficially doesn't cause issues as in INSS templates you don't really get to build stacks, + airlift allows repositioning an entire spawn elsewhere. The culprit is \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=40937994}{here}, in the ability to airlift armies and gift to the opponent on the same turn, introducing too much variance.
\\
\\
Recent attempts to use this card involve \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer/Tournament?ID=58382}{GMELD} and \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer/Tournament?ID=61583}{Platcasso}. The idea behind the templates is that you can only hold a certain amount of cards, making it so that you must use the airlift whenever you gather the pieces or on Turn 1.
