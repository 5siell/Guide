\subsection{Order Priority}

Order Priority guarantees for you first move in a specific turn. It is generally an incredibly strong card, that only gets stronger at reverse kill-rates. 
\\
\\
You can find a lot of scenarios that justify it's usage which really narrow down to 3 ideas
\begin{itemize}
    \item Dodging an opponent's stack to survive somewhere \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=21225771}{Turn 12 by Beep}. Kind of a desperation move and sometimes high risk but useful in most vanilla and WR templates.
    \item Sniping an opponent's stack of armies before they move / eliminating them. \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=33424922}{Turn 8}. Straight forward idea.
    \item Getting Better kill-rates \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=31218324}{Turn 7}. The idea of using order priority to get a beneficial trade is mostly applicable to timid lands and fast earth. However, it can occassionally be helpful in every template
    \item Controlling move order. An idea behind this is explained also in \href{https://www.warzone.com/Forum/149405-trans-attc-percentage-cards?Offset=0}{Frog's tips}. A niche use of this comes with using the card, and attacking the opponent on your 2nd and 3rd attack, so they go in the same cycle. Another idea with this comes at using the order priority card to move last. For example in \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=37803798}{this Fast Earth game} on Turn 4, I use the card to make sure that a) my first move snipes his armies in Greenland and b) my 2nd move will come after his capture in Indonesia.
\end{itemize}
