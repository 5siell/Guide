Bomb

There are no MTL templates featuring the Bomb Card. However, it used to be in Georgia AC back in whenever. It is the heart of one of the most popular templates, Small Earth ComBomb. The presence of the Bomb card comes with the fear to the opponent that it can be used at any time to cripple any army advantage. As far as I know it’s a heavily unpopular card. It recently has been used in Malvia 2vs2 as a way to get a +3 FTB on turn 1 by bombing a neutral, or as a way to push for elimination, again on turn 1. It follows the concept that you need to play or discord the card on turn 1, effectively balancing it, since the variance comes from the threat that the opponent can play it anytime, not the execution.
