\section{Rounding}

This section is for me asking Optimum every other week whether sanction rounds up or down.  I'll try to include every rounding interaction here for fast tracing. \href{https://www.warzone.com/Forum/230870-rounding-5-reference}{Forum} for reference.

\subsection{Sanctions}

Depends how you see it.

\begin{itemize}
    \item The fractional penalty rounds DOWN.
    \item Overall it rounds Up.
    \item \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=36855257}{Turn 5}. Rufus sanctions PS.
    One way to look at it:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item PS was at 10 income before sanctions
        \item Sanctions hit 25\%. -> 2.5. PS' income rounded to 10-2.5=7.5
        \item 7.5 becomes 8, Sanctions round UP.
    \end{enumerate}
    Another way to look at it:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item PS was at 10 income before sanctions
        \item Sanctions hit 25\% -> 2.5. The fractional penalty of 2.5 rounds to 2. It rounds DOWN.
        \item Final income is 10-2=8
    \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}

In the Forum it says it rounds down, to me it always rounded up. You can find tables for sanctions on the Cards section.

\subsection{Reinforcement Card}

Rounds DOWN

\begin{itemize}
    \item Exception: Anything less than 1 army always rounds UP, you don't get a rf of +0.
    \item RF Card Rounds DOWN.
    \item No example for it, in Tarabonia's Switch it's 40\%, no 0.5.
    \item 50\% RF for 5 turns =2.5 -> +2 RF card.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Emergency Blockade \& Blockade}

Rounds DOWN

\begin{itemize}
    \item \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=41361948}{Turn 6}. 250\% blockade at 3 armies. 3->7.5. Rounds DOWN to 7. Same for Emergency Blockade!
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Bomb}

Rounds DOWN

\begin{itemize}
    \item Bombing 5 armies -> 5 * 50\% = 2.5 -> Rounds to 2. Kills Extra.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Kill Rates}

Rounds UP
\begin{itemize}
    \item 5 Defenders at 70\% kill rate kill 3.5 -> 4 attackers.
    \item 2 Attackers at 75\% kill rate kill 1.5 -> 2 defenders.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Attack by Percentage}

Rounds DOWN

\begin{itemize}
    \item 5 movable armies and 50\% attack -> 5* 50\% = 2.5 -> Rounds down to 2.
\end{itemize}
