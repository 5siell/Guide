These six templates generally follow the same recipe but with small spicing factors on niche things. You’ll find that they all come at Base Income of 5 and 4 armies per Initial Territory, which implies the combos are the same across all templates. Nonetheless, take note of the following main difference between the six templates.

  • British Raj revolves around it’s 4 efficient bonuses, has 4 spawns, and a 1-time use of a recon card.

  • Tor-ture:

    1. Largest size (in Territories and Bonuses).

    2. Compartmentalized map into income regions to cover.

    3. Least coverage provided from Initial Territories per Map size & Least Ratio of Wastelands to Bonuses →\rightarrow no perfect pick-set, no way to cover everything.

    4. No blockade card.

  • Strat ME and Final Fantasy VII are the same exact thing but in a different map.

  • Randomized Strat ME is a unique idea with bonuses fluctuating between being super-efficient and not efficient at all.

  • Phobia is the middle child:

    1. Really small map, that’s only smaller due to having +1 bonuses.

    2. Almost every bonus is equally efficient so in reality it has as much variety, if not more, than Strat ME.

    3. The map has a handful of bottlenecks. Less than ME.
