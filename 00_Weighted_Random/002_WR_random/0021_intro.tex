These six templates generally follow the same recipe but with small spicing factors on niche things. You'll find that they all come at Base Income of 5 and 4 armies per Initial Territory, which implies the combos are the same across all templates. Nonetheless, take note of the following main difference between the six templates.
\begin{itemize}
    \item British Raj revolves around it's 4 efficient bonuses, has 4 spawns, and a 1-time use of a recon card.
    \item Tor-ture:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Largest size (in Territories and Bonuses).
        \item Compartmentalized map into income regions to cover.
        \item Least coverage provided from Initial Territories per Map size \& Least Ratio of Wastelands to Bonuses $\rightarrow$ no perfect pick-set, no way to cover everything.
        \item No blockade card.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Strat ME and Final Fantasy VII are the same exact thing but in a different map.
    \item Randomized Strat ME is a unique idea with bonuses fluctuating between being super-efficient and not efficient at all.
    \item Phobia is the middle child:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Really small map, that's only smaller due to having +1 bonuses. 
        \item Almost every bonus is equally efficient so in reality it has as much variety, if not more, than Strat ME.
        \item The map has a handful of bottlenecks. Less than ME.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
