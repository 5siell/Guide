\section*{Abstract} \label{abstract}

Hi, my name is Chris. I first joined this game back in 2016 with 2 friends. Only started playing this game more frequently and got active with clans and ladders around the summer of 2023. Therefore, if you are reading this document, take into consideration that for the longest period of Warlight's (nowadays called Warzone's) history, I was absent. The MTL changed hands and was re-enabled on Christmas 2023 (\href{https://www.warzone.com/Forum/734956-join-multitemplate-ladder-mtl}{Forum}), by the joined effort of some very important individuals, mentioned in the Introduction \ref{introduction}. Ever since then, I had wanted to compile a guide; more so an archive of information. Not everything went exactly according to plan, but I got help along the way that motivated me tremendously. I cannot thank those people enough. Even if their contribution may seem small or useful only in one template, I will always be in debt to them for giving me the incentive to finish it and advocating in peer review. 
\\
\\
Around summer of 2024 I came back to this and slowly wrote more and more as I understood the game more proficiently, given whatever free time and motivation I could find. The reason was, surprisingly for some, that I was baffled by the lack of public guides. From a community-focused perspective, hoarding guides, or information (especially beginner friendly ones) appears to me as a short-sighted attempt at gaining a temporary competitive edge as a clan-individual. When a new player joins a complex and strategy-heavy game with no official tutorial or guide, it is easy to get lost or overwhelmed. If they cannot find resources, there is a higher chance they'll quit. On the other hand, from a competitive standpoint, by sharing guides, you expand the pool of skilled players, ultimately raising the skill ceiling for everyone. People who learn fast will push the meta even further, benefiting all competitive players by introducing new tactics.
\\
\\
Of course, the argument that a strategic scene can become stagnant if most players hoard knowledge is an exaggerated take on things and most definitely not 100\% correct, as players are resourceful and can learn by observation. Evidently, I got really invested into this game, so I took it upon myself to concentrate all the information I have gathered in a single document. All these, guides, forums, documentation, may seem excessive but they make sure information does not vanish when people who developed it are gone or move on to other pursuits. I have found myself opening this document whenever I want to lookup a template-specific setting or just something that I always seem to forget, like whether the sanction rounds up or down. Hopefully, others will find it useful.
\\
\\
The goal, as of this moment in time (January 2025), includes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Documenting every single MTL template and it's play-style.
    \item Serving as a repository for useful information.
\end{enumerate}

This guide will not magically get you to the top of the ladder, myself I never reached the top of the ladder. Bare that in mind.
