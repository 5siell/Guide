\subsection{Intel Move}

This regards the forum \href{https://www.warzone.com/Forum/652358-warzone-secret-want-know}{post}. The idea is straight-forward. The game doesn't know whether or not a move will fail before queueing it. As a result you can que a 0-move at the end of your moves in order to potentially gain information on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The move order
    \item The amount of delays your opponent did
\end{itemize}

The most useful experience I had with it was in this \href{https://www.warzone.com/MultiPlayer?GameID=36761733}{Clan League 3vs3 game} where RynoFury attacked on his 6th move north in Partha, telling me that they have a teammate somewhere in the region that made it so he got a bonus with a tap.
