Intel Move

This regards the forum post. The idea is straight-forward. The game doesn’t know whether or not a move will fail before queueing it. As a result you can que a 0-move at the end of your moves in order to potentially gain information on:

  • The move order

  • The amount of delays your opponent did

The most useful experience I had with it was in this Clan League 3vs3 game where RynoFury attacked on his 6th move north in Partha, telling me that they have a teammate somewhere in the region that made it so he got a bonus with a tap.
